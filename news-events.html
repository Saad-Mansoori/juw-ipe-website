News & Events

Stay up to date with the latest happenings, workshops, and professional development opportunities at IPE.

News & Events

28 & 29 September 2026

SME Launch Pad Program

"Empowering Aspiring Entrepreneurs With Practical Knowledge For Building And Growing Sustainable Businesses."

The SME Launch Pad Program was organized in collaboration with the State Bank of Pakistan. The program provided aspiring entrepreneurs with practical insights into entrepreneurship, business development, financial awareness, and opportunities within the SME sector. The initiative supported participants in strengthening their entrepreneurial knowledge and understanding the fundamentals of developing sustainable business ventures.

9 September 2026

Organic Soap Making Workshop

"Exploring Natural Product Development through Organic Soap Making."

An Organic Soap Making Workshop was organized to introduce participants to the fundamentals of handmade soap production. The session covered ingredients, formulation, preparation techniques, and safe handling practices. Participants gained insight into natural product development and the potential for transforming creative skills into small-scale entrepreneurial opportunities.

8 September 2026

Basic Crocheting Workshop

"Learning the Art of Crocheting through Practical and Creative Skill Development."

A Basic Crocheting Workshop was conducted to introduce participants to fundamental crocheting techniques. The session covered basic stitches, crochet tools, and materials while providing participants with an opportunity to develop a practical creative skill. The workshop also highlighted the potential of handicraft skills for personal development and entrepreneurial opportunities.

7 September 2026

Canvas Painting Workshop

"Inspiring Creativity and Artistic Expression Through Canvas Painting."

A Canvas Painting Workshop was organized to provide participants with an opportunity to explore their creativity through painting. The session introduced basic canvas painting techniques, composition, and artistic expression. Participants actively engaged in the creative process and developed their skills in a supportive and interactive learning environment.

2, 3 & 5 September 2026

Microbiology for a Safer Food Industry: Quality, Safety & Sustainability

"Strengthening Knowledge of Microbiology For Safer, Higher-Quality, And Sustainable Food Production."

A professional training program titled "Microbiology For A Safer Food Industry: Quality, Safety & Sustainability" was conducted to strengthen participants' understanding of microbiology in the food sector. The program focused on food quality, safety, hygiene, contamination control, and sustainable food production. The sessions provided valuable knowledge and an industry-oriented understanding of microbiological practices.

22 August, 29 August & 5 September 2026

Industrial Quality Assurance and Regulatory Laboratory Practices

"Developing Industry-Relevant Knowledge In Quality Assurance, Quality Control, And Regulatory Laboratory Practices."

A three-day professional training program on Industrial Quality Assurance and Regulatory Laboratory Practices was conducted to strengthen participants' understanding of quality systems and laboratory standards. The sessions covered quality assurance, quality control, laboratory practices, HACCP, risk management, traceability, and regulatory requirements. The program provided valuable industry-oriented knowledge relevant to modern laboratory and quality management practices.

24 August 2026

Block & Screen Printing Workshop

"Exploring Traditional Printing Techniques through Creative And Practical Learning."

Jinnah University for Women organized a Block & Screen Printing Workshop as part of its professional and skill-development initiatives. The workshop introduced participants to traditional and contemporary printing techniques, including design development, fabric selection, and printing methods. The session provided a creative learning experience and encouraged participants to develop practical skills with potential for personal and entrepreneurial applications.
